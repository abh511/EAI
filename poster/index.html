Project Title : “ EnviroWatch AI — AI-Powered Environmental Monitoring & Decision Support System ”

Final Year B.E. Project  |  Department of Computer Engineering  |  Academic Year 2025–26
Aim : To develop an AI-powered real-time environmental monitoring system for Indian cities (Pune & Mumbai) that tracks 7 environmental pillars, predicts risks, and supports urban decision-making.
Objectives :
1
Monitor air, water, noise, industrial emissions, solid waste, urban heat & groundwater in real-time across 20 city locations.
2
Classify environmental risk levels (Low / Medium / High / Critical) using an ML ensemble model (Random Forest + XGBoost).
3
Provide 48-hour forecasts, anomaly detection, construction site suitability analysis, and a citizen grievance reporting portal.

This project follows an Applied Research & Experimental Development methodology. Phase 1 — existing environmental monitoring systems, smart city frameworks, and Indian pollution datasets (CPCB, MPCB) were studied to identify gaps. Phase 2 — a structured dataset of 10,000 records across 70 parameters was compiled for 20 locations in Pune and Mumbai, cleaned using median imputation and normalized with StandardScaler. Phase 3 — an ensemble ML model (Random Forest + XGBoost, soft voting, 80/20 split, 5-fold CV) was trained for risk classification; Isolation Forest was trained separately for anomaly detection. Phase 4 — all models were integrated into a FastAPI backend, connected to React 18 frontend and Supabase, and validated through unit, API, and end-to-end testing.
